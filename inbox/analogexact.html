VIBE Hysteresis Character:

406: Warmest. Early soft saturation, rounded transients, vintage "pillow" compression.

456: Reference. Balanced warmth, classic "tape sound" - warm but defined.

499: Crisp. Most headroom, sharp transient attack, tight punchy bass.

GP9: Ultra-clean. Sharpest transients, most transparent, mastering-grade.

SM900: Transparent. Strong bass, open top end, European character.

SM911: Smooth. Cleaner than 456 in low-mids, less "bulbous" bottom.
